## How I Work Best
- I do my best work when **I have clear tasks and well-defined goals** — knowing exactly what I'm aiming for helps me move forward confidently.
- I struggle when **tasks are vague and I'm unsure of the direction.** If I go quiet on something, it often means I'm not sure where to start, so a quick bit of clarity helps me a lot.

## Communication Style
- I prefer messages to be **short and direct** — I'd rather have the key point up front than a long lead-up.
- The best way to reach me is:
  - **Email** for general, non-urgent things.
  - **SMS** when it's more urgent and you need a quicker reply.
  - **A phone call** for anything genuinely urgent.
  - For everyday async team chat, **Discord** works too.
- If you need something urgent, **send me an SMS or call** rather than waiting on email.

## Collaboration & Teamwork
- I work best in a team when **everyone has clear roles and there's a shared plan**, so we all know what we're responsible for.
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is **unclear direction** — not knowing what the goal is makes it hard to contribute well.
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means **I'm processing and thinking things through**, not that I'm disengaged or upset.

## Focus & Productivity
- I stay focused by **using a structured to-do list** — breaking things down and working through them keeps me on track.
- Things that distract me: **sudden, unexpected message interruptions** tend to break my focus the most.
- The best way to support my productivity is **to let me have some uninterrupted focus time**, and to batch non-urgent messages rather than pinging me constantly.

## Feedback & Growth
- I like feedback to be **direct and straightforward** — clear and to the point works best for me.
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is **to point it out directly, focused on the actions and not the person** — no personal attacks, just what to fix and how.
- I appreciate it when teammates **are honest and clear with me**, and give me the context I need to improve.
